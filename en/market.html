HOLY MARKET · 2026.12.20

A Christmas Holy Market
to share together

On the Fourth Sunday of Advent, parish groups and neighbors open booths and invite the whole neighborhood. Funds raised by sharing go toward preparing for WYD 2027.

FIXED · 2

Info desk · WYD booth

The info desk handles information, coordination, accounts, lost and found, and first aid. The WYD booth introduces World Youth Day and raises funds.

EXPERIENCE · 4

Activity zone

Rosary bracelets, nanoblock crosses, sock-loop Christmas ornaments and a wish and prayer card tree.

SNACK · 6

Snack zone

One booth per menu — café (hot chocolate, coffee, ssanghwa tea), mulled wine, bakery (cookies, cupcakes) and three hot-snack booths (hot dogs and steamed buns / tteokbokki and fish cakes / pancakes). Choose a menu when you apply.

MARKET

Market zone

The religious goods shop and local small businesses, donated crafts such as Christmas wreaths, parish group booths and outside vendor booths.

Booth availability

Google Form applications are added in order once the organizer has checked them. Each item is limited to 2 booths, so please check which items are already taken before applying.

How it works

FIRST COME

Groups, first come first served

Each parish group gets one booth, assigned in order of application. Note your preferred zone (activity, snack or market) and items when applying.

ITEMS

Max 2 booths per item

For the same item or theme, the group that applies first has priority, up to 2 booths. Items for sale should be new.

OPEN TO NEIGHBORS

Market zone open to outside vendors

Once group booths are set, remaining market-zone spots are opened to local residents and small businesses (11.15–11.25). The same-item rule applies and parish groups have priority.

SHARING

Only rankings are published

After the event we announce only the ranking of booth donations, not the amounts. Proceeds go to the WYD 2027 fund.
