OpenHOMESTAY

Homestay host families wanted

Our four-week Advent drive is under way. Welcome pilgrims from around the world into your home during the main event. No special facilities are needed — a simple place to sleep and a light breakfast are enough.

  • Sign-up: Saturday vigil Mass of the First Sunday of Advent (11.28) – Christmas Eve (12.24)
  • How: at church after Masses from the Saturday vigil to the Sunday 6 pm young adult Mass (except early-morning Mass), or with a form from the parish office
  • Dates: Monday, August 2 – Sunday, August 8, 2027
  • Requirements: a parishioner household, room for 2 or more pilgrims, a light breakfast

OpenHOLY MARKET

Christmas Holy Market booths

A Christmas market on December 20 (Fourth Sunday of Advent) where parish groups and neighbors run activity, snack and market booths. Proceeds go to the WYD 2027 fund.

  • When: Sunday, December 20, 09:00–16:00, Meeting Room and in front of the small chapel
  • Parish group booths: November 8 – 25, first come, first served
  • Outside vendors (market zone): November 15 – 25
