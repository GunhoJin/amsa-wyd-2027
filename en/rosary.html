How to take part

5 decades = 1 bead

PARISH

Parish box

Put one bead in the Rosary box at the Amsa-dong Parish office for every five decades. The parish counts them weekly and reports to the archdiocese, and in 2027 the beads will be made into real rosaries.

PERSONAL

Hasang app · website

Anyone can log the decades they pray in the Hasang app or on the WYD website. Online entries are limited to 25 decades per 2 hours.

Offer online

GROUP

Group offering

Groups other than parishes offer their decades through a representative: apply for a group account and, once it is approved the next day, log decades with it.
