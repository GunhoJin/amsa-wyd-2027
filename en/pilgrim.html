PILGRIMAGE · SEOUL

The martyrs’ road,
walked by young feet

These routes follow in the footsteps of the martyrs of the Korean Church. In 2018 the Holy See proclaimed them Asia’s first official international pilgrimage site, and the WYD Seoul 2027 organizing committee has walked them with young people in its pilgrimage programs.

Bukchon Pilgrimage Walk

Bukchon’s faith history through its people

Walk through Insadong and Bukchon following the stories of the Heungseon Daewongun, Fr. James Zhou Wenmo — the first foreign priest martyred in Korea — and Columba Kang Wan-suk, who hid him.

Seosomun Pilgrimage Walk

From Myeongdong to the Seosomun Martyrs’ Shrine

Starting at Myeongdong Cathedral, where the first Korean Catholic community was born, the walk passes through Jeong-dong to the Martyrs’ Shrine outside Seosomun Gate and Yakhyeon Catholic Church.

Han River Pilgrimage Walk

From Mapo to Jeoldusan and Yanghwajin

From Mapo Food Street, past the Tojeong riverside gateway, the walk follows dirt, stone, forest and flower paths along the river to Jeoldusan Martyrs’ Shrine, site of the Byeongin Persecution martyrdoms, and Yanghwajin, where foreign missionaries rest.

Meeting point
Mapo Station, Exit 1 (Line 5)

Gold numbers are sites of the Seoul Catholic Pilgrimage Routes. These are guided programs and must be booked in advance.

Cost Guiding is free (admission, activity and transport costs are your own). Tours are also available in English, Japanese, Chinese, Vietnamese, Malay, Indonesian, Thai and sign language.

Compared with the routes The three guided walks are sightseeing courses that add nearby landmarks to some of the pilgrimage sites. The Bukchon walk shares only the Gwanghwamun Beatification Site, Gahoe-dong Catholic Church and Seokjeong Boreum Well (end of the Way of the Word to start of the Way of Life); the Seosomun walk shares Myeongdong Cathedral, the Seosomun Martyrs’ Shrine and Yakhyeon Catholic Church (start of the Way of the Word, end of the Way of Life); and the Han River walk shares only Jeoldusan Martyrs’ Shrine (Way of Unity). Danggogae, Saenamteo, Samseongsan and the other shrines on the Way of Unity are not included. Use the three courses above for pilgrimage, and the guided walks for first-time visitors or young pilgrims from abroad.

Booking Individuals up to 3 days ahead (next month’s bookings open on the 15th), groups 5 months to 5 days ahead. Recommended for upper elementary age and up; children under 14 without a guardian will be turned away.

Cancellation Canceled during weather warnings such as heat waves, typhoons, heavy rain or fine dust; no tours in peak summer (7.23–8.11). Inquiries: Seoul Walking Tours +82-2-6925-0777

Pilgrimage tips

ORDER

Three connected courses

The Way of Life begins at Gahoe-dong Catholic Church where the Way of the Word ends, and the Way of Unity begins at Yakhyeon Catholic Church where the Way of Life ends. You can walk one course a day.

DISTANCE

Distance by course

The downtown Way of the Word (8.7 km) and Way of Life (5.9 km) take half a day each; for the Way of Unity (29.5 km), which links the shrines along the Han River, allow a day or more using public transport.

WYD

Youth pilgrimage program

In the 2025 ‘September Companions’ program, the organizing committee had young people walk these routes collecting stamps in a pilgrim passport, then offer the passports at the Mass honoring the martyrs at Jeoldusan.
