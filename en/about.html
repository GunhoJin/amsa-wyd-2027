ABOUT · WYD SEOUL 2027

Young people of the world
are gathering in Seoul

World Youth Day, the Catholic Church’s festival where young people from around the world gather with the Pope, will be held in Seoul in the summer of 2027.

Main event2027.8.3 – 8.8
Host citySeoul, Korea
Theme verseJohn 16:33
In AsiaFirst since Manila 1995

INVITATION

The Pope’s invitation

“The next World Youth Day will take place in Asia: in Seoul, Korea.”Pope Francis · Sending Mass of WYD Lisbon, August 6, 2023

The Pope described the move from Lisbon, at the western edge of Europe, to Seoul in the far East as a sign of the dream of the Church’s universality and unity. Then on August 3, 2025, Pope Leo XIV announced the dates of the Seoul event at the closing Mass of the Jubilee of Youth in Rome.

THEME

Theme verse

“Take courage! I have conquered the world”John 16:33

These are the words Jesus left his disciples at the Last Supper. They recall the victory of Christ, who overcame fear even in the face of his Passion and death. They speak not of mere optimism but of hope and courage rooted in the living Christ.

The hope of this World Youth Day is that young people facing conflict, job insecurity and economic hardship will meet one another in Seoul, experience the joy of the Gospel, and be sent into the world as ‘pilgrims of hope’ and ‘missionaries full of courage’.

The themes of the last four World Youth Days form a single journey.

LOGO

Logo and colors

The official logo writes the name of the capital, ‘Seoul’ (서울), in traditional Korean calligraphy. The rhythmic brushstrokes of the Korean letters can also be read as the English letters W·Y·D, expressing the harmonious unity of Seoul and WYD, East and West. Its theme is ‘Seoul and the youth of the world, united in the Holy Spirit’.

PATRONS

Patron saints

St. John Paul II

1920–2005Feast Oct 22Symbol · Crosier

He founded World Youth Day in 1984, inviting young people everywhere to be one in faith. He visited 129 countries during his pontificate and was canonized in 2014.

St. Andrew Kim Taegon, priest, and companion martyrs

1821–1846Symbol · Red stole

The first Korean Catholic priest. He was martyred at Saenamteo at the age of 25, just a year after his ordination, and was canonized in Seoul in 1984 with the 103 Korean Martyrs.

St. Frances Xavier Cabrini

1850–1917Feast Dec 22Symbol · Steamship

Despite frail health she crossed the Atlantic 30 times and founded 67 institutions for immigrants. Today the patron saint of immigrants, she is a model of hospitality.

St. Josephine Bakhita

1869–1947Feast Feb 8Symbol · Broken chains

A ‘witness of hope’ who, through God’s love, regained dignity and freedom amid the suffering of slavery. Her feast is also the World Day of Prayer against Human Trafficking.

St. Carlo Acutis

1991–2006Feast Oct 12Symbol · Computer

A young saint known as the ‘patron saint of the internet’. He shared the faith digitally through a website cataloguing Eucharistic miracles and was canonized in 2025.

ECOLOGY

WYD for the Earth

To live out the event’s core value of ‘love and ecology’, the organizing committee is running the ‘Breath of Life for the Whole World’ (Onsum) campaign. In the spirit of the encyclical Laudato Si’, it is a movement to care for the Earth as our common home.

Together with the Seoul Metropolitan Government and the Korea Forest Service, six tree-planting events are held each spring and autumn from 2025 to 2027, with about 2,000 young people planting more than 10,000 trees. The last one is on Arbor Day, April 5, 2027.
